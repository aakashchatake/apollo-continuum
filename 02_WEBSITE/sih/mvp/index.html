Why partial irrigation?

The control trajectory reaches the caution zone first. Hydrogel creates a finite root-zone water buffer, so the system proposes a smaller timed cycle rather than a full fixed schedule.

Model assumptions

Hydrogel stores only part of the base irrigation pulse and releases it when moisture falls. Biochar modifies water-loss behaviour; it is not treated as the same material. Results require calibration against controlled plots.
