# AgriIntel Scope and Apollo Handoff Boundary

## Position in Apollo

```text
Apollo - umbrella identity
|
|-- Apollo AgriVerse - agricultural intelligence world
|   |
|   |-- Mission I - historical Apollo AgriGuard research record
|   |-- Mission II - current internship contribution
|   |   `-- AgriIntel - crop and field intelligence vertical slice
|   |
|   `-- Intelligent Soil - separate SIH-linked decision workbench
|
`-- Apollo Robotics - ARGUS and future autonomous systems
```

## What the AgriIntel Team Owns

The team owns a bounded internal project contribution:

- a structured crop or field observation record;
- data-quality checks and evidence labels;
- one explainable baseline analysis flow;
- a dashboard and reusable demo scenarios;
- tests, setup instructions and a documented handoff.

## What the AgriIntel Team Does Not Own

- the Apollo brand, company platform, commercial product direction or production infrastructure;
- the Mission I archive or original contributor attribution;
- the SIH Intelligent Soil implementation or its external student-team deliverables;
- farmer data, field trials, agronomic validation, material claims or final recommendations;
- a new cloud deployment unless explicitly approved through the company process.

## Connection to the Intelligent Soil MVP

The public AgriIntel page may link to the Intelligent Soil MVP as a related decision workbench. That is a navigation and learning connection, not a code merge or an evidence transfer. The AgriIntel team should not report SIH results as its own, and the SIH team should not report AgriIntel work as its own.

## Handoff Rules

1. Keep the active code in the registered repository and retain commit history.
2. Maintain a short evidence note: what was built, what was tested, what is simulated and what remains unverified.
3. Submit dependencies, data sources, licences and model files for review before integration.
4. Any reusable component moves to a company-controlled repository only after technical review.
5. Student contribution remains credited; product ownership and production deployment remain with Chatake Innoworks Pvt. Ltd.

## One Sentence for the Team

> Build one trustworthy field-intelligence decision loop that Apollo can review and reuse; do not try to rebuild the entire Apollo AgriVerse ecosystem.
