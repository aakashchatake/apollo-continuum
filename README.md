# Apollo Continuum

Apollo Continuum is the public ecosystem layer for Apollo: the historical foundation connecting research, intelligent systems, agriculture, livestock, robotics, publication, production, and future initiatives.

It is not a replacement for the legacy Apollo project, MindforgeAI, IJAIE, Apollo Production, or Apollo AgriVerse. It is the parent narrative and discovery layer above them.

## Workspace

- `00_DISCOVERY_AND_GOVERNANCE`: source inventory, content decisions, gaps and evidence rules.
- `01_REGISTRY`: central structured records for initiatives, projects and destinations.
- `02_WEBSITE`: local-first public website prototype. No production deployment or DNS change is included.

## Canonical Boundaries

- Apollo is the umbrella identity and historical foundation.
- Apollo Production and Apollo AgriVerse are separate branches.
- ORIGIN belongs to Apollo Production.
- AgriIntel and PashuSense belong to Apollo AgriVerse.
- ARGUS belongs to Apollo Robotics.
- IJAIE remains a separate historical publication property.
- MindforgeAI is the detailed engineering/evidence layer for current project representations.
- Nori/Room 212 materials are private foundation-history sources, not public website content.
- Chatake Innoworks Pvt. Ltd. is the legal owner.
