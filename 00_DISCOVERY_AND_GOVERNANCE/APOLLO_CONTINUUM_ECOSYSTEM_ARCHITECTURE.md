# Apollo Continuum Ecosystem Architecture

Apollo Continuum is the parent public layer. It carries the identity, history, cross-domain logic and future direction. Individual systems retain their own operational homes.

```text
APOLLO CONTINUUM
|
|-- Research and journal ecosystem
|   |-- IJAIE flagship and named journal catalogue
|   |-- InnoWorks Press publishing identity
|   `-- evidence-aware research records
|
|-- Intelligence worlds
|   `-- Apollo AgriVerse
|       |-- AgriIntel
|       |-- PashuSense
|       `-- Intelligent Soil / Digital Twin
|
|-- Autonomous systems
|   `-- Apollo Robotics -> ARGUS
|
|-- Creation
|   `-- Apollo Production -> ORIGIN
|
|-- Foundation and people
|   `-- historical formation, mentorship and future programmes
|
`-- Engineering evidence layer
    `-- MindforgeAI project records and applications
```

The system deliberately keeps **narrative ownership** in Apollo Continuum and **implementation evidence** in the appropriate project and MindforgeAI records.
