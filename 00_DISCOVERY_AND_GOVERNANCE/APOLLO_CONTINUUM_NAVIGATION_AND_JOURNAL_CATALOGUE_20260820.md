# Apollo Continuum Navigation and Journal Catalogue Update

Date: 2026-08-20

## Decision

Apollo Production and Journals are primary, standalone header destinations. They are not hidden under the general Explore menu.

Apollo Production remains the public production-house page for the creative branch. ORIGIN is represented only as confidential development; no screenplay, story, theme, character, world-building or other internal material is exposed.

## Journal Position

The Apollo Continuum journal page presents IJAIE as a distinct editorial ecosystem within Apollo's intellectual and publication continuum. It does not rebrand IJAIE, copy journal content, or represent editorial roles as Apollo employment.

InnoWorks Press is the separate publishing identity. Chatake Innoworks Pvt. Ltd. remains the legal owner.
